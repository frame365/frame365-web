How do I restore my Frame365 Premium purchase?

Open Frame365, go to the You tab, open the Premium upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How does iCloud backup work?

Frame365 automatically syncs your photos, captions, and locations to your private iCloud container when iCloud Drive is enabled on your iPhone. Your memories are never uploaded to public servers.

Why isn't my Home Screen widget updating?

After adding a daily frame, iOS refreshes widgets automatically. If a widget appears delayed, open Frame365 once or remove and re-add the widget on your Home Screen.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

All subscriptions are managed through Apple. Open your iPhone's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > Frame365 to modify or cancel your plan anytime.
