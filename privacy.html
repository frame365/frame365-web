Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Frame365 ("we", "our", or "us") is built around a simple principle: your daily memories belong exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy explains how Frame365 handles your information when you use our iOS application and website.

1. Data Storage & iCloud Sync

Frame365 does not operate external user accounts or store your photos, captions, or journal entries on our own servers. All photos, notes, and metadata you create are stored locally on your device and, if enabled, synced privately through your personal Apple iCloud account.

2. Camera, Photo Library & Location Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used solely to capture or select your daily photo and save generated Story posters when you choose to export them.
  • Location Data: Used only when you capture or edit a frame to tag your memory with the city and place you visited. Location data stays private on your device and iCloud.
  • Face ID / Passcode: Processed entirely on-device via Apple's LocalAuthentication framework to lock your journal. We never receive biometric data.

3. Subscriptions & Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Apple App Store and managed via RevenueCat using anonymous app user identifiers. We do not collect or store your credit card or billing details.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Tracking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, photos, or location history to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

5. Data Deletion

Because your journal data resides on your device and personal iCloud, you have complete control over it. You can delete individual frames inside the app at any time or remove all app data by deleting the app and clearing its iCloud container in iOS Settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at frame365.app@gmail.com.
